# Lessons for KOML

The domain requires an explicit snapshot date and separate follow-up dates. A past follow-up date does not imply an open proposal: accepted, declined and withdrawn statuses must exclude it from the queue. Proposal face value is not earned revenue.

Validated within synthetic evidence: deterministic integer arithmetic and exact-set comparisons reconcile all 200 planted cases. The corrupted-record test removes one overdue proposal and reduces its amount total by exactly 10,000 minor units. This validates computation, not the assumption that real outreach data will be complete or that users will adopt the tool. Real usefulness remains unmeasured.

Evidence-based improvement: compare exact recipient IDs as well as totals, and deliberately corrupt one record. Matching totals alone could hide wrong classifications. This improvement is exercised by the zero-false-positive and corruption tests.

Operating rule for future builds: make date, currency and provenance explicit before computing; reject invalid records atomically, and defer a business verdict until measured use exists.
